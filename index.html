    About

    Privacy

    Everything happens in your browser. Your videos are read from your disk and never uploaded: the page's Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none') blocks every network request, and there are no analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts and fonts. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees that you loaded it, like any website.

    Your decisions and thumbnails are saved in this browser, for this site only. Clearing your browsing data erases them. Your videos are not affected.

    Deleting

    Delete marked... permanently deletes the videos you marked, after you confirm. They don't go to the Recycle Bin. The browser asks you to allow changes to the folder first.

    Browsers

    Chrome, Edge and most Chromium browsers: everything works and your folders are remembered. After restarting the browser you may have to allow access again; pick "allow on every visit" if it's offered. If yours shows the read-only message anyway, it has the File System Access API turned off.

    Firefox, Safari: watch and mark only. Export the list and delete the files yourself. You have to pick your folders again on each visit.

    Playback depends on what your browser can decode: H.264 MP4 works everywhere, HEVC or MKV may not.
